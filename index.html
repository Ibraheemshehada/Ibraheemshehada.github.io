About

Featured · 2026 · Health AI · Technical lead

DiaFootCare

An iOS app, published on the App Store, that monitors diabetic foot wounds from phone photographs. It tracks healing over time and uses computer vision to identify tissue types and estimate wound size, with a calibration reference in the frame that turns pixels into real millimetres.

Under the hood: a U-Net with a MobileNetV2 encoder and attention for segmentation, a CLIP ViT-B/32 encoder with SVM heads for five tissue classes, and fp16 TFLite models that run offline on the phone. The calibration-ring detector is classical computer vision, kept analytic so clinicians can audit every measurement.

● Clinical trial NCT07815275 · Oman MoH · 139 participants ↗
